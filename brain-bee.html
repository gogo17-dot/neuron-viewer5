Brain Bee preparation

The International Brain Bee is a prestigious neuroscience competition hosted each year for teenagers all around the globe. Students start at a local chapter, winners advance to a national championship, and national champions compete at the IBB World Championship. Local chapters set their own formats — confirm rules with your coordinator.

  1. 1 · Local Chapter contests. Format varies by organiser.
  2. 2 · National Country championship. Winner qualifies for the world stage.
  3. 3 · IBB World Neuroanatomy, written test, patient diagnosis, then live judging.
