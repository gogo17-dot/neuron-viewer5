Privacy

Privacy

Short version: AXON has no accounts, no cookies and no ads, and it does not sell or share data. You can use every page without telling us who you are.

Counting visits

  • We count page visits with GoatCounter, an open-source counter that sets no cookies and does not track you across sites.
  • It records only general information: which page was opened, the referring site, browser type, screen size and country. It does not store your IP address or identify you.
  • Visits are only counted on axon.az itself, and most ad blockers switch the counter off — that is fine with us.

What stays on your device

  • Your language, day/night theme, game results (Beynle, Lesion hunter) and practice progress are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device, and we cannot see them.
  • If you install AXON as an app, pages are stored on your device so they work offline.
  • To delete all of this, clear the site data for axon.az in your browser settings.

When you send us something

  • The “Propose a work” form sends your name, email and message to us through Web3Forms, an email delivery service, or through your own email app. We use them only to reply and to review your proposal.
  • If you email us, we keep the email only as long as we need it to answer you.

Other services the pages load

  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, and the 3D and chart libraries from the jsDelivr and cdnjs networks. Like any website, these services receive your IP address when they deliver the files.
  • Lesson videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and load only when you open them.
  • The homepage shows the badges of the Netty and MilliNet awards; the MilliNet badge is loaded from millinet.az.
  • Links to other sites (PubMed, Instagram, Facebook and others) follow those sites’ own privacy rules.
