About NEYRON

NEYRON exists to make neuroscience more popular and easier to learn. It is a bilingual atlas where you can turn 3D structure, read short clinical notes beside the models, explore MRI-style scans, and keep papers and open textbooks in one place — so more people can see how the nervous system works.

Who it is for

Students, educators, publishers, researchers, and curious readers with a strong interest in neuroscience and neurobiology. The UI stays bilingual so learners in Azerbaijan and readers abroad can use the same atlas.

What you will find

  • Atlases — 3D viewers for neuron, synapse, meninges, cortex, brainstem, pathways, and more.
  • Brain Bee prep — anatomy and function quizzes with 3D atlas links, plus a patient-diagnosis drill.
  • Brain scans — MRI-style exploration with region context.
  • Articles — a curated shelf with original commentary and legal access links only.
  • Books — open textbooks you can read here when redistribution is allowed, plus link-only sources when it is not.
  • Publish — propose a paper, book, or 3D atlas structure for review.

How proposals are reviewed

Publish is a review workflow, not an open upload. You can propose a research article, a book, or a 3D atlas structure. Submit the details and licence answers (articles and books also include a short original summary). Next, the site author receives the proposal, checks accuracy and rights, then decides whether the proposed work gets published on this site.

Author

NEYRON is built and curated by Omar Garagozov.

Licences & teaching use

We host open textbooks only when the licence clearly allows redistribution (Creative Commons or public domain), with attribution. Several hosted titles are NonCommercial (NC) — while those files are on NEYRON, this site does not carry advertising or sell products tied to them. Free-to-read works without redistribution rights stay as external links. Article summaries are original commentary, not publisher abstracts.
